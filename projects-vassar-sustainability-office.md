# Vassar Office of Sustainability: Electricity Analytics

**Tools:** R, time-series analysis, data visualization, Shiny

## Project Overview

Analyzed campus electricity consumption, demand, and emissions to investigate usage patterns and identify potential data-quality issues.

## Approach

- Explored electricity data and investigated anomalies.
- Used statistical and time-series analysis to examine consumption patterns.
- Developed an interactive R Shiny dashboard to communicate findings.
- Considered how data limitations and operational factors affected interpretation.
